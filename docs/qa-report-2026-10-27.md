# QA report: Lev HaBinyan demo (Track A)

Date: 2026-10-27. Target: https://lev-habinyan-so33.vercel.app (branch `track-b-whatsapp-bot`, commit d60d6c1).
Method: 30 personas. Each persona walks the app and the v1 scope ([v1-scope.md](../../v1-scope.md)).

## How to read this report

Each finding has a tag for its evidence:

- **[LIVE]**: I saw it on the deployed site (desktop and 375 px phone view, read-only).
- **[CODE]**: I read it in the source. I did not run it.
- **[SCOPE]**: The v1 scope promises it. The app does not show it.
- **[TEST]**: I did not test it. Someone must test it before the pilot.

I did not create, edit or delete any data. All write flows are marked [TEST] or [CODE].

Severity: **S1** blocks the demo or the pilot. **S2** hurts daily work. **S3** is polish.

## Summary

The 30 personas gave 45 findings (section 2) and 10 scope gaps (section 3).
Most S1 items sit in three places: the demo access, the Today screen order, and the missing WhatsApp and committee parts.
Section 5 lists the order of work.

## 1. The 30 personas and what each found

### Founders and dispatchers

**P1. Roi, founder and dispatcher (daily user)**
- The Today screen lists six overdue maintenance items before the open faults. The only `סכנה` fault (exposed wire, 105 hours without action) is the first row of the fault block at the bottom. [LIVE] S1
- He cannot set a visit time, a responsibility, or a resident contact on a ticket from the ticket page. The scope lists these in 3.2. [SCOPE] S2
- Created tickets have no reporter. A fault he enters for a resident has no phone number to call back. [CODE] S2

**P2. Omer, founder (second dispatcher)**
- The app greets everyone with `בוקר טוב, רועי`. The demo user is mapped to Roi. In the demo, every action logs as Roi. [LIVE] S2
- The assignee list mixes founders, employees and suppliers in one flat list, with no groups. [LIVE] S3

**P3. New hire dispatcher (week one)**
- There is no help text, no empty-state guide and no tour. The status list has seven values and no explanation (`הוקצה`, `ממתין`, `נפתח מחדש`). [LIVE] S3
- Any status can move to any other status (for example `בוטל` to `בוצע`). [CODE] S2

**P4. Onboarding manager (loads the first 5 buildings)**
- `/import` accepts only a CSV of the control sheet. There is no import for residents, units or suppliers. [LIVE] S2
- No sample CSV or column guide is shown on the import page. [LIVE] S3
- Many cells show `?` (no data). The scope says this is correct. A manager who sees a grid of `?` can think the product is empty. [LIVE] S3

**P5. On-call dispatcher (night, danger alert)**
- The scope says `danger` sends a WhatsApp and SMS alert at once. I found no alert code, no on-call number setting, and no webhook route in the app. [SCOPE] S1
- `/settings` has only the user list and one email toggle. [LIVE] S1 (same item)

### Field staff

**P6. Yossi, building handyman (one phone, many buildings)**
- Employees have no login or screen. They use WhatsApp only. The WhatsApp bot code exists in `src/lib/bot`. No route receives its webhook, so nothing can run. [CODE] S1
- The ticket list has a `שלי` filter, but the handyman cannot sign in. [LIVE] S3

**P7. Miriam, cleaner (low digital skill)**
- She gets no jobs, no reminders and no way to mark `done` with a photo. [SCOPE] S2

**P8. Electrician supplier (receives a job)**
- A supplier has no page, no link and no message. The ticket shows only a name in a list. [LIVE] S2
- No field for quote, visit window or warranty on a ticket. [LIVE] S3

**P9. Elevator supplier (inspection, SLA)**
- The supplier list shows `SLA` as a label. No timer or breach flag uses it. [LIVE] S2

### Residents and committee

**P10. Elderly resident (phone calls, no WhatsApp)**
- A resident who does not use WhatsApp has no way to report. The dispatcher can create a ticket, but cannot store the caller as the reporter. [CODE] S2

**P11. Russian or Arabic speaking resident**
- The app is Hebrew only. The bot code has Hebrew texts only. The scope asks for Arabic, Russian and English input. [SCOPE] S2

**P12. Angry resident (reports the same fault twice)**
- No duplicate detection exists in the app. The scope lists it in 3.1 item 5. [SCOPE] S2

**P13. Tenant (renter) and P14. Non-resident owner**
- No resident register is visible in the app. The building page was not opened in detail. [TEST]
- No owner or tenant field to route responsibility. [SCOPE] S2

**P15. Committee chair**
- No committee page and no magic link. The scope promises a read-only page per building (3.4). [SCOPE] S1
- Without it, the managers cannot show the value to the committee, who pay.

**P16. Committee member (65+, phone)**
- Same gap as P15. [SCOPE]

### Money, legal, safety

**P17. Bookkeeper**
- A ticket has one cost field and one invoice file. There is no expense list, no export, and no total per building. [LIVE] S2
- The cost field shows `עלות (₪)` and a save button. Negative numbers and very large numbers are not limited. [CODE] S3
- Costs for `תשלום לגנן` look like a task, but there is no payment reminder state. [LIVE] S3

**P18. Insurance agent**
- `חידוש ביטוח מבנה` is a ticket with no building. There is no documents area per building to store the policy. The scope lists documents (3.2). [SCOPE] S2

**P19. Safety inspector (lift and fire dates)**
- Dates are free to edit. There is no history of who changed a due date. [CODE] S3
- The scope says the lift default is every 6 months. I did not check what the app sets. [TEST]

**P20. Privacy lawyer (Amendment 13)**
- No consent record, no opt-out and no privacy text exist in the app. They belong to Track B. [SCOPE] S2
- The demo uses one shared account. There is no access log. [LIVE] S2

### Quality and access

**P21. Screen reader user**
- The nav links have names. Each grid cell has an `aria-label` (system and state). I read this in the code, so I did not log it as a finding. [CODE] Pass
- The status colours are paired with icons. This is good for colour blind users. [LIVE]

**P22. Low vision user (large text)**
- The maintenance grid needs horizontal scroll on a phone. The last column (`חש…`) is cut at the edge. There is no cue that the grid scrolls. [LIVE] S2

**P23. Android phone on a weak mobile network**
- The first load of `/demo` does a server sign-in and a redirect. [TEST] S3
- The logo is a 107 KB PNG. The manifest uses it as the app icon with a wrong size claim (480x480). [CODE] S3

**P24. iPhone user (add to home screen)**
- The manifest has one icon. iOS needs an `apple-touch-icon`. [CODE] S3

**P25. Security tester**
- The anon key sees no rows. Row-level security works for the probe I ran (`person`). [LIVE] Pass
- The cron endpoint returns 401 without the secret. [LIVE] Pass
- Only the HSTS header is set. No CSP, no `X-Frame-Options`, no `X-Content-Type-Options`, no `Referrer-Policy`. [LIVE] S2
- `/demo` signs in anyone who opens the link. The whole app and the database are open. A search engine or a link preview bot that follows the URL gets a session. [LIVE] S1 for production, accepted for the demo
- `/demo` is a GET with a side effect. A prefetch can trigger it. [CODE] S3
- No rate limit on `/demo`. [CODE] S3

**P26. DevOps (deploys and secrets)**
- The deploy comes from a manual `create_deployment` on a feature branch. A push to `main` makes a build without the demo login. [LIVE] S2
- The service role key and the cron secret are not set. Daily reminders do not run. [LIVE] S2
- Preview and production use the same Supabase project. [LIVE] S2

### Business and edge cases

**P27. The managers who watch the demo**
- First screen is red: six overdue items and five untreated faults. This shows the product is useful, but a demo with no green state does not show "success". [LIVE] S3
- The sign-out button on `/settings` leads to `/sign-in`. The Google button there cannot work (the provider is not set). The page does not show the `?error=1` message. [LIVE/CODE] S1 for the demo
- Unknown URLs redirect (307) instead of a 404 page. [LIVE] S3

**P28. Pilot building (real data day)**
- The seed building names have 6 units each. Real buildings have 40 or more. The units screen was not tested at that size. [TEST]
- Typed unit numbers create a unit if it does not exist. `5`, `05` and `5א` can become three units. [CODE] S2

**P29. Shabbat and holiday planner**
- Due dates use the Gregorian calendar. No Hebrew date, no holiday or Shabbat check. A due date on Shabbat gets the same reminder. [SCOPE] S3
- The daily reminder runs at 05:00 UTC (08:00 or 07:00 in Israel, by season). [CODE] S3

**P30. Clumsy user (double taps, back button, bad input)**
- Status changes read the old value, then write. Two founders who change the same ticket at the same time can overwrite each other. [CODE] S3
- A double tap on `שמירה` in the new ticket form may create two tickets. [TEST] S2
- Long titles, emoji, and Arabic or Russian text in a title were not tested. [TEST]
- Upload types are limited to images, PDF, Office and text, and size to 10 MB. The server action body limit is 12 MB. Phone photos of 5 MB pass. [CODE] Pass

## 2. Findings by area (merged list)

### Demo and access
1. S1. Sign-out leads to a dead end. Fix: hide the sign-out button in demo mode. Or make `/sign-in` show `/demo` as a button.
2. S1. `/demo` opens the app to anyone. Fix before real data: remove `DEMO_*` variables, or add a shared secret in the link.
3. S1. A push to `main` breaks the demo. Fix: merge `track-b-whatsapp-bot` into `main`, or set the production branch.
4. S2. The demo user is Roi. Fix: show a neutral name in demo mode.
5. S2. Preview deployments share the production database.
6. S3. Unknown URLs give a redirect, not a 404 page.

### Today screen
7. S1. Order of blocks. Danger and urgent faults must come first. Fix: a top block `דחוף עכשיו`.
8. S2. The faults block shows no assignee and no `לא שויך` badge. The ticket list does. [LIVE]
9. S2. Counters: `היום 0` and `באיחור 6` count only maintenance. `פתוחות 6` is not the same as the five open faults in the list. The meaning is unclear. [LIVE]
10. S2. No link from a counter to its filtered list. [TEST]
11. S3. Grammar: `1 תקלות`. Use singular and plural forms.
12. S3. The `9 אין נתונים` badge on a building looks like a count of problems.

### Tickets
13. S1. No ticket can be linked to a resident (reporter). See P1 and P10.
14. S2. No visit time, no responsibility, no due time on the create form. The edit sheet may have them. [TEST]
15. S2. The new ticket form has defaults (`חשמל`, `חשוב`) already selected. A hurried user saves the wrong category.
16. S2. No building is pre-selected, and the form does not remember the last one.
17. S2. No status rules. Add allowed transitions, and ask for a reason on `בוטל`.
18. S2. `בוצע` needs no photo. The scope asks for one on resident faults.
19. S2. Double submit risk (P30).
20. S3. Overdue label: `105 שעות ללא טיפול`. Show days after 48 hours.
21. S3. No bulk actions (assign many, close many).
22. S3. No timeline of messages with the resident (Track B).

### Maintenance calendar
23. S2. The grid hides columns on a phone. Add a scroll cue, or a list view per building.
24. (Withdrawn.) The grid cells already have an `aria-label`.
25. S2. No reminder test. Email reminders are off until a sender exists.
26. S3. Legend and filters are visible, but the grid does not use them. [TEST]
27. S3. No due date history.

### Buildings, people, suppliers
28. S2. No resident register visible (name, phone, unit). [TEST]
29. S2. Supplier list has no search, no filter by category, no call button. [LIVE]
30. S2. Employees have no phone number shown, so no one can call them. [LIVE]
31. S2. Unit number duplicates (P28).
32. S3. The supplier list repeats category as a heading, and the tier as a badge. Fine, but dense.
33. S3. No documents per building.
34. S3. No notes on a building (gate code, key location). This is the most requested field in similar tools.

### Security and privacy
35. S2. Add security headers (CSP, frame, nosniff, referrer policy).
36. S2. No audit log of reads. The scope removed it from v1. State this to the founders.
37. S2. Service role key and `CRON_SECRET` not set. Set them before the pilot.
38. S3. `/demo` GET side effect and no rate limit.
39. S3. Email sign-up on Supabase must be off for production (see README). I could not check it with the tools I have. [TEST]

### Accessibility and devices
40. (Withdrawn, same as 24.)
41. S2. Horizontal scroll cue (P22).
42. S2. Contrast of the grey `אין נתונים` icon on the light grey cell. [TEST]
43. S3. Apple touch icon, manifest icon size.
44. S3. No dark mode. [LIVE]
45. S3. The desktop view uses a side rail. The ticket page on desktop leaves a large empty area. [LIVE]

## 4. Ideas that the scope does not list (worth a talk with the founders)

1. A phone-call log. Many residents call. A "log a call" button creates a ticket with the caller.
2. A building notes field (gate code, key box, caretaker phone).
3. A "send to supplier" button. It opens WhatsApp with a ready message and the ticket link.
4. A weekly summary for the founders: new, closed, overdue, by building.
5. A Hebrew date and Shabbat warning on due dates.
6. A read-only share link for one ticket, for a supplier.

## 5. Recommended order of work

Before the manager demo (small changes):
1. Put danger and urgent faults first on the Today screen (finding 7).
2. Hide or fix the sign-out button in demo mode (1).
3. Fix `1 תקלות` and the `9 אין נתונים` badge (11, 12).
4. Add a scroll cue to the maintenance grid (23, 41).
5. Merge the demo branch into `main` (3).

Before the pilot:
6. Deploy the WhatsApp webhook and the danger alert (missing 1, 2).
7. Add reporter, visit time, responsibility to the ticket (13, 14).
8. Build the committee page (missing 3).
9. Set the service role key and the cron secret (37).
10. Remove the demo account. Turn on Google sign-in. Turn off email sign-up (2, 39).
